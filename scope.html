What this tool does

Move Maker evaluates the risk of extending or renegotiating a contract for a player at the club they currently play for. It looks at proposed wage and contract length against a player's age, injury history, market value, and historical peer data to estimate contribution, retention, and value risk.

What this tool does not do

The tool does not evaluate a player for a club they don't currently belong to. This is an intentional guardrail, not a missing feature: allowing any club to be paired with any player would open the door to using the tool for new-signing or acquisition scouting, which is out of scope for this project.

If a player's league is outside the tool's coverage, or there's no record of them at the selected club, the tool returns a plain refusal message instead of a dashboard.

Why it matters

Keeping the tool scoped to a player's actual, current club keeps the output grounded in real, verifiable contract history rather than speculative what-if scenarios.
